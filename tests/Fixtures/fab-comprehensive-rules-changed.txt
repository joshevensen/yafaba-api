1 Preface
Flesh and Blood is a competitive trading card game where two heroes face off in a fight to the death.
The rules in this document apply to any game of Flesh and Blood and supersede any previous version.
1 Game Concepts
1.0 General
1.0.1 The rules in this document apply to any game of Flesh and Blood.
1.0.1a If an effect directly contradicts a rule contained in this document, the effect supersedes that rule, unless a tournament rule says otherwise.
Example: If a card's text says otherwise, the card's text wins.
1.0.2 A restriction is a rule or effect that states something cannot happen.
1.0.3 A requirement is a rule or effect that states that something should happen if possible.
1 Glossary
1.-1.2 (2H)
A subtype of a weapon object that is equipped to two weapon zones.

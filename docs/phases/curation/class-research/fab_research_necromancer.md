## Class Identity

**Mechanical theme:** Not covered in fabtcg.com's Jump Start article, and this is currently the thinnest roster in the dataset (only 1 SAGE-legal hero: Gravy Bones, who is dual-class Pirate/Necromancer — meaning **Necromancer currently has zero SAGE-legal heroes of its own** that aren't also primarily Pirate). Frankie, Make Ends Meat (equipment-graveyard recursion) is CC-only and not SAGE-legal at all.

**This is about to change.** Confirmed via fabtcg.com/product-releases: **Malice**, a new Shadow-talent Necromancer, arrives with the set **Usurp the Shadow Throne** (September 25, 2026) and gets her own **Armory Deck: Malice** (September 18, 2026). Unlike Gravy Bones, Malice is a standalone Necromancer — not dual-class. Official flavor text confirms the mechanical identity: *"Flesh and Blood's latest Necromancer uses Shadow to twist zombies into corrupted corpses, sending them back into battle even after they have decayed beyond use."* This is a reanimation/recursion theme — undead allies that keep coming back after dying, consistent with the graveyard-recursion pattern already seen in Gravy Bones and Frankie. Silver Age support for the new Usurp the Shadow Throne cast is stated to follow the main set release, though a Malice-specific Chapter Deck isn't confirmed yet as of this research pass — worth rechecking closer to launch.

**Recurring patterns (current roster):** graveyard/banished-zone equipment recursion (Frankie) and blue-card-to-graveyard payoffs (Gravy Bones' watery grave line) suggest a "the graveyard is a resource" identity — and Malice's confirmed zombie-recursion theme reinforces that this is the class's throughline, not a coincidence of a thin sample.

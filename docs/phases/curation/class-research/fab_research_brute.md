## Class Identity

**Mechanical theme:** Per fabtcg.com's Jump Start article: *"Some players love to feel the rush of risky plays when they are in the thick of a game. Brute is the perfect class for that type of player... You rarely forget the first time you miss banishing a 6 power attack in Levia and taking a ton of blood debt damage."* High variance is baked directly into the class's own cards — Kayo, Berserker Runt literally rolls a die to double or halve an attack's power. The recurring "6 or more power" threshold across nearly every Brute hero here reflects the class's identity around big, singular, risky swings rather than many small ones.

**Recurring patterns:** the "6+ power" threshold appears on almost every hero (Kayo lines, Rhinar, Tuffnut), and the crowd-response subsystem (Kayo Strong-arm, Tuffnut) is shared with Guardian's showman heroes — both classes draw from the same Pit-Fighter arena flavor.

## Ability Text

**Kayo:**
> You have 1 weapon zone.  
> Attack action cards you own get +1{p} while they are in any zone other than the combat chain.  
> The first time you discard a card with 6 or more {p} during each of your action phases, create a Might token.

**Kayo, Armed and Dangerous:**
> You have 1 weapon zone.  
> Attack action cards you own get +1{p} while they are in any zone other than the combat chain.  
> The first time you discard a card with 6 or more {p} during each of your action phases, create a Might token.

**Kayo, Berserker Runt:**
> Whenever you play an attack action card with base power 6 or more {p}, roll a 6 sided die. On;  
> - 1 to 4 - Halve the attack's base {p}, rounded down.  
> - 5 to 6 - Double the attack's base {p}.

**Kayo, Strong-arm:**
> You start the game with 1 weapon zone.  
> **Instant** - {r}{r}{r}{r}, {t}: Target attack action card you control has 6 base {p}.  
> Whenever the crowd boos you, create a Vigor token.

**Kayo, Underhanded Cheat:**
> You start the game with 1 weapon zone.  
> **Instant** - {r}{r}{r}{r}, {t}: Target attack action card you control has 6 base {p}.  
> Whenever the crowd boos you, create a Vigor token.

**Levia:**
> If a card with 6 or more {p} has been put into your banished zone this turn, cards you own lose **blood debt** during the end phase.

**Levia, Shadowborn Abomination:**
> If a card with 6 or more {p} has been put into your banished zone this turn, cards you own lose **blood debt** during the end phase.

**Rhinar:**
> Whenever you discard a card with 6 or more {p} during your action phase, **intimidate**.

**Rhinar, Reckless Rampage:**
> Whenever you discard a card with 6 or more {p} during your action phase, **intimidate**.

**Tuffnut:**
> **Instant** - {t}: Pitch the top card of your deck. If it has 6 or more {p}, **the crowd cheers** you.  
> Whenever the crowd cheers you, create a Toughness token.

**Tuffnut, Bumbling Hulkster:**
> **Instant** - {t}: Pitch the top card of your deck. If it has 6 or more {p}, **the crowd cheers** you.  
> Whenever the crowd cheers you, create a Toughness token.

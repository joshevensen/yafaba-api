## Class Identity

**Mechanical theme:** Per fabtcg.com's Jump Start article: *"We see this with Ninja heroes too. The head equipment, Mask of Momentum, awards the Ninja player with a card draw for hitting the opposing hero three times in a row... A savvy Ninja player can present a flurry of attacks that make it hard for the opponent to have that good timing."* Fast, sequenced attack chains are the core identity — several heroes here reward the **second attack** specifically (Ira) or search for **combo**-keyword cards mid-turn (Katsu, Zen), which is a distinctly Ninja mechanic: tutoring a specific payoff card out of your deck once a condition is met.

**Recurring patterns:** combo-card tutoring (Katsu, Zen), Draconic-graveyard recursion (Cindra's daggers, Fai's Phoenix Flame) — Ninja overlaps heavily with Draconic talent in the young hero pool.

## Ability Text

**Benji, the Piercing Wind:**
> Your attack action cards with 2 or less {p} can't be defended by cards from hand.  
> The first time an attack action card you control hits each turn, your next attack gains +1{p}.

**Cindra:**
> Whenever you hit a **marked** hero, create a Fealty token.  
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {r}{r}{r}: Equip up to 2 Draconic daggers from your graveyard. This costs {r} less to activate for each Draconic chain link you control.

**Cindra, Dracai of Retribution:**
> Whenever you hit a **marked** hero, create a Fealty token.  
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {r}{r}{r}: Equip up to 2 Draconic daggers from your graveyard. This costs {r} less to activate for each Draconic chain link you control.

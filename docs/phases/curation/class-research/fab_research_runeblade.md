## Class Identity

**Mechanical theme:** Per fabtcg.com's Jump Start article and Ultimate Guard's strategy guide: *"the Runeblade class uses a combination of non-attack actions and attack actions to present both physical and arcane damage"* and *"can create huge stacks of runechant tokens, letting you send them all at the opponent in one turn."* The **Runechant token** is the class's core resource (Viserai, Vynnset), built by playing non-attack actions alongside attacks — forcing the opponent into genuinely hard blocking decisions since they don't know if a hit is "just" physical or about to trigger a Runechant arcane burst.

**Recurring patterns:** Runechant token generation (Viserai, Vynnset), Embodiment token generation tied to elemental talents (Aurora/Lightning, Briar/Earth+Lightning, Florian/Earth) — Runeblade overlaps heavily with the Elemental talent family in this dataset.

## Ability Text

**Aurora:**
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {r}{r}: Create an Embodiment of Lightning token. Activate this only if you've played a Lightning card this turn.  
> **Essence of Lightning**

**Aurora, Emissary of Lightning:**
> **Instant** - {r}{r}, {t}, destroy a Lightning Flow you control: Create an Embodiment of Lightning token.

**Aurora, Legacy of Tempest:**
> **Instant** - {r}{r}, {t}, destroy a Lightning Flow you control: Create an Embodiment of Lightning token.

**Aurora, Shooting Star:**
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {r}{r}: Create an Embodiment of Lightning token. Activate this only if you've played a Lightning card this turn.  
> **Essence of Lightning**

**Briar:**
> **Essence of Earth and Lightning**  
> The first time an attack action card you control deals damage to an opposing hero, create an Embodiment of Earth token.  
> Whenever you play your second 'non-attack' action card each turn, create an Embodiment of Lightning token.

**Briar, Warden of Thorns:**
> **Essence of Earth and Lightning**  
> The first time an attack action card you control deals damage to an opposing hero, create an Embodiment of Earth token.  
> Whenever you play your second 'non-attack' action card each turn, create an Embodiment of Lightning token.

**Chane:**
> **Once per Turn Action** - Create a Soul Shackle token: Your next Runeblade or Shadow action this turn gains **go again. Go again**

**Chane, Bound by Shadow:**
> **Once per Turn Action** - Create a Soul Shackle token: Your next Runeblade or Shadow action this turn gains **go again. Go again**

**Florian:**
> If there are 4 or more Earth cards in your banished zone, Florian gets "If you would create 1 or more aura tokens, instead create that many plus 1 of each of those tokens."  
> **Essence of Earth**

**Florian, Rotwood Harbinger:**
> If there are 8 or more Earth cards in your banished zone, Florian gets "If you would create 1 or more aura tokens, instead create that many plus 1 of each of those tokens."  
> **Essence of Earth**

**Viserai:**
> Whenever you play a Runeblade card, if you have played another 'non-attack' action card this turn, create a Runechant token.

**Viserai, Rune Blood:**
> Whenever you play a Runeblade card, if you've played another non-attack action card this turn, create a Runechant token.

**Vynnset:**
> At the start of your turn, banish a card from your hand. If you do, create a Runechant token.  
> Whenever you play a Shadow non-attack action card, you may pay {h}. If you do, the next Runechant effect that would deal damage this turn can't be prevented.

**Vynnset, Iron Maiden:**
> At the start of your turn, banish a card from your hand. If you do, create a Runechant token.  
> Whenever you play a Shadow non-attack action card, you may pay {h}. If you do, the next Runechant effect that would deal damage this turn can't be prevented.

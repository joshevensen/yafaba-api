## Class Identity

**Mechanical theme:** Per fabtcg.com's Jump Start article: *"Assassins like Arakni and Uzuri... have the ability to push damage through with a combination of attack reactions and daggers that make it harder to block."* **Stealth** is the class's core keyword — nearly every hero here has an ability that keys off attacks with stealth (a keyword that restricts which cards can defend against the attack), reinforcing an identity built around making the opponent's blocking decisions worse, not just attacking harder.

**Recurring patterns:** stealth-attack payoffs (all three Arakni variants, Nuu), and a mark-hero synergy shared with Runeblade/Ninja's "marked hero" template (Arakni, Marionette/Web of Deceit both care about marked opponents, echoing Fang and Cindra's Draconic dagger kits in other classes).

## Ability Text

**Arakni:**
> Whenever you play a card with **contract**, you may look at the top card of target opponent's deck. You may put it on the bottom.

**Arakni, 5L!p3d 7hRu 7h3 cR4X:**
> The first attack with **stealth** each turn gets **go again**.

**Arakni, Huntsman:**
> Whenever you play a card with **contract**, you may look at the top card of target opponent's deck. You may put it on the bottom.

**Arakni, Marionette:**
> Your attacks with **stealth** that are attacking a **marked** hero get +1{p} and "When this hits, this gets **go again**."  
> At the beginning of your end phase, if an opponent is **marked**, you become a random Agent of Chaos.

**Arakni, Solitary Confinement:**
> Your first attack with **stealth** each turn has **go again**.

**Arakni, Web of Deceit:**

**Nuu:**
> Your attacks with **stealth** get "When this chain link resolves, banish all action cards defending this."  
> **Instant** - {c}{c}{c}: Look at the top card of an opposing hero's deck. If it's blue, you may banish it. Until end of turn, you may play blue cards from that hero's banished zone without paying their {r} cost.

**Nuu, Alluring Desire:**
> Your attacks with **stealth** get "When this chain link resolves, banish all action cards defending this."  
> **Instant** - {c}{c}{c}: Look at the top card of an opposing hero's deck. If it's blue, you may banish it. Until end of turn, you may play blue cards from that hero's banished zone without paying their {r} cost.

**Uzuri:**

If it's an attack action card with cost 2 or less, put target attacking card with **stealth** from the active chain link on the bottom of its owner's deck, then put the banished card onto the active chain link as the attacking card.

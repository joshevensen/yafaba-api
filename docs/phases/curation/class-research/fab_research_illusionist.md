## Class Identity

**Mechanical theme:** Per fabtcg.com's Jump Start article: *"Illusionist heroes do this in a much different way by gradually building up a presence of auras or allies in the arena with the end goal of overwhelming their opponent."* Token/aura accumulation is the throughline — Spectral Shield tokens (Enigma, Prism), Ash tokens (Dromai), and holo-counter aura recursion (Zyggy) all build a persistent board state over several turns rather than a fast burst.

**Recurring patterns:** heavy overlap with the soul zone (Prism's two versions both banish from soul), and talent diversity is high in this class — Illusionist hosts Draconic (Dromai), Mystic (Enigma), Light (Prism), and Lightning (Zyggy) talent heroes, more talent variety than most other classes in this dataset.

## Ability Text

**Dromai:**
> Whenever you pitch a red card, create an Ash Token.  
> If you've played a red card this turn, dragons you control have **go again** while attacking.

**Dromai, Ash Artist:**
> Whenever you pitch a red card, create an Ash Token.  
> If you've played a red card this turn, dragons you control have **go again** while attacking.

**Enigma:**
> Your first Spectral Shield attack each turn costs {r} less to activate.  
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {c}{c}{c}: Create a Spectral Shield token with a +1{p} counter.

**Enigma, Ledger of Ancestry:**
> Your first Spectral Shield attack each turn costs {r} less to activate.  
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {c}{c}{c}: Create a Spectral Shield token with a +1{p} counter.

**Enigma, New Moon:**
> Equipment you own gets **cloaked**  
> **Instant** - {c}{c}{c}: Turn target face-down equipment you have equipped face-up. If it has **ward**, create 3 Spectral Shield tokens.

**Prism:**
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {r}{r}, banish a card from Prism's soul: Create a Spectral Shield token.

**Prism, Advent of Thrones:**
> Whenever a card with Herald in its name is put into Prism's soul during an action phase, you may search your deck for a figment, put it into the arena, then shuffle.  
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {r}{r}, banish a card from Prism's soul: **Awaken** target figment you control.

**Prism, Awakener of Sol:**
> Whenever a card with Herald in its name is put into Prism's soul during an action phase, you may search your deck for a figment, put it into the arena, then shuffle.  
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {r}{r}, banish a card from Prism's soul: **Awaken** target figment you control.

**Prism, Sculptor of Arc Light:**
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {r}{r}, banish a card from Prism's soul: Create a Spectral Shield token.

**Zyggy:**
> **Instant** - {r}{r}, {t}, destroy a Lightning Flow you control, banish another Lightning aura permanent you control with no holo counters: Return the banished aura to the arena with a holo counter.

**Zyggy Starlight:**
> **Instant** - {r}{r}, {t}, destroy a Lightning Flow you control, banish another Lightning aura permanent you control with no holo counters: Return the banished aura to the arena with a holo counter.

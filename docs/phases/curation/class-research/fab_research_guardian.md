## Class Identity

**Mechanical theme:** Defense-first control. Per fabtcg.com's Jump Start article: *"Oldhim, the Elemental Guardian, slowly controls the game with defense cards and taxing effects in an effort to whittle down the opponent's resources and cards to win."* The class rewards patient, methodical play — several heroes here (Oldhim, Jarl, Terra, Florian's cousin-class patterns) use the **Essence of [Element]** mechanic, which triggers off having specific pitch colors in hand/banished, rewarding a controlled, color-conscious hand.

**Recurring patterns:** "Essence of X" abilities (Oldhim, Jarl, Terra), crowd-response mechanics tied to a Pit-Fighter/showman sub-theme (Bravo, Lyath, Pleiades, Tuffnut all reference "the crowd" — see also Brute, which shares this theme), and wager/gold-token economy (Betsy, Victor Goldmane).

**Type identity:** Guardian is the largest class in this dataset by hero count (20 playable entries after filtering Mentor/Pit-Fighter types) — it has the deepest roster of any class covered here.

## Ability Text

**Betsy:**
> Whenever an attack you control **wagers**, you may pay {r}{r}. If you do, the attack gets +1{p} and **overpower**.

**Betsy, Skin in the Game:**
> Whenever an attack you control **wagers**, you may pay {r}{r}. If you do, the attack gets +1{p} and **overpower**.

**Bravo:**
> **Action** - {r}{r}: Until end of turn, your attack action cards with cost 3 or more get **dominate**. **Go again**

**Bravo, Flattering Showman:**
> **Action** - {r}{r}, {t}: Turn a face-down card in your arsenal face-up. If it has crush, it gets +2{p} and **dominate** this turn. **Go again**

**Bravo, Showstopper:**
> **Action** - {r}{r}: Until end of turn, your attack action cards with cost 3 or greater gains **dominate**. **Go again**

**Bravo, Star of the Show:**
> **Essence of Earth, Ice, and Lightning**  
> At the start of your turn, you may reveal an Earth, an Ice, and a Lightning card from your hand. If you do, the next attack action card with cost 3 or greater you play this turn gains +2{p}, **dominate**, and **go again**.

**Brevant, Civic Protector:**
> You may have any number of Chivalry in your deck.  
> Whenever you **protect** another hero, create a Might token.

**Jarl Vetreiði:**
> Whenever you play an Ice card, create a Frostbite token in an opponent's exposed head, chest, arms, or legs zone.  
> **Essence of Earth and Ice**

**Lyath Goldmane:**
> The base {p} and {d} of cards you control are halved, rounded up.  
> **Instant** - {r}{r}, {t}: **The crowd boos** you. Defending action cards you control get +1{d} this turn.  
> Whenever the crowd boos you, create a Might token.

**Lyath Goldmane, Vile Savant:**
> The base {p} and {d} of cards you control are halved, rounded up.  
> **Instant** - {r}{r}, {t}: **The crowd boos** you. Defending action cards you control get +1{d} this turn.  
> Whenever the crowd boos you, create a Might token.

**Oldhim:**
> **Essence of Earth and Ice**  
> **Once per Turn Defense Reaction** - {r}{r}{r}: If an Earth card is pitched this way, prevent the next 2 damage that would be dealt to Oldhim this turn. If an Ice card is pitched this way, the attacking hero puts a card from their hand on top of their deck.

**Oldhim, Grandfather of Eternity:**
> **Essence of Earth and Ice**  
> **Once per Turn Defense Reaction** - {r}{r}{r}: If an Earth card is pitched this way, prevent the next 2 damage that would be dealt to Oldhim this turn. If an Ice card is pitched this way, the attacking hero puts a card from their hand on top of their deck.

**Pleiades:**
> **Instant** - {t}, remove a suspense counter from an aura you control: You may put a suspense counter on an aura of suspense you control.  
> Whenever the crowd cheers you, create a Confidence token.

**Pleiades, Superstar:**
> **Instant** - {t}, remove a suspense counter from an aura you control: You may put a suspense counter on an aura of suspense you control.  
> Whenever the crowd cheers you, create a Confidence token.

**Terra:**
> At the beginning of each end phase, if there is an Earth card in your pitch zone, you may pay {r}. If you do, create a Might token.  
> **Essence of Earth**

**Valda Brightaxe:**
> Whenever an opponent draws a card during an action phase, create a Seismic Surge token for each card drawn this way.  
> At the start of your turn, if you control 3 or more Seismic Surge tokens, cards you own with **crush** gain **dominate** this turn.

**Valda, Seismic Impact:**
> Whenever an opponent draws 1 or more cards during an action phase, create that many Seismic Surge tokens.  
> At the start of your turn, if you control 3 or more Siesmic Surge tokens, cards you own with **crush** get **dominate** this turn.

**Victor Goldmane:**
> The first time each turn you create a Gold token from an effect you control, draw a card.  
> The first time each turn you would fail to win a **clash**, instead you may destroy a Gold you control. If you do, put 1 of the revealed cards on the bottom of its owner's deck, then **clash** again.

**Victor Goldmane, High and Mighty:**
> The first time each turn you create a Gold token from an effect you control, draw a card.  
> The first time each turn you would fail to win a **clash**, instead you may destroy a Gold you control. If you do, put 1 of the revealed cards on the bottom of its owner's deck, then **clash** again.

**Yoji, Royal Protector:**
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {r}{r}{r}: The next time another target hero would be dealt damage this turn, instead that damage is dealt to Yoji and prevent 1 of that damage.

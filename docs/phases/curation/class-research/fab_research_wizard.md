## Class Identity

**Mechanical theme:** Per fabtcg.com's Jump Start article: *"Wizards like Kano and Iyslander want to do one thing only - wait until the right moment to sling spells at their opponent until their life reaches 0."* Instant-speed flexibility is the throughline — Kano and Oscilio both play cards from the top of the deck as instants, Iyslander plays arsenal cards as instants off-turn, and Blaze converts "opt" effects into banked energy for a delayed arcane burst. This is the class most defined by *timing* rather than raw stats.

**Recurring patterns:** "play this as though it were an instant" shows up on nearly every hero here (Kano, Oscilio both lines, Blaze), and Emperor, Dracai of Aesir is dual-class here and in Warrior (see Warrior research file) — flag for cross-reference.

## Ability Text

**Blaze, Firemind:**
> Whenever you **opt**, put energy counters on Blaze equal to the number of cards looked at this way.  
> **Once per Turn Instant** - Remove X energy counters from Blaze: Banish a Wizard non-attack action card from your hand with an effect that deals arcane damage equal to X. You may play it this turn as though it were an instant.

**Emperor, Dracai of Aesir:**
> You may only have red cards in your deck.  
> **Action** - {r}{r}{r}: Search your deck for Command and Conquer, **attack** with it, then shuffle.

**Iyslander:**
> **Essence of Ice**  
> If it's not your turn, you may play blue 'non-attack' action cards from your arsenal as though they were an instant.  
> Whenever you play an Ice card during an opponents turn, create a Frostbite token under their control.

**Iyslander, Stormbind:**
> **Essence of Ice**  
> If it's not your turn, you may play blue 'non-attack' action cards from your arsenal as though they were an instant.  
> Whenever you play an Ice card during an opponents turn, create a Frostbite token under their control.

**Kano:**
> **Instant** - {r}{r}{r}: Look at the top card of your deck. If it's a 'non-attack' action card, you may banish it. If you do, you may play it this turn as though it were an instant.

**Kano, Dracai of Aether:**
> **Instant** - {r}{r}{r}: Look at the top card of your deck. If it's a 'non-attack' action card, you may banish it. If you do, you may play it this turn as though it were an instant.

**Oscilio:**
> **Once per Turn Instant** - Discard an instant: Draw a card.  
> **Essence of Lightning**

**Oscilio, Constella Intelligence:**
> **Once per Turn Instant** - Discard an instant: Draw a card.  
> **Essence of Lightning**

**Oscilio, Forked Continuum:**
> **Instant** - {r}, {t}, destroy a Lightning Flow you control: Discard a card and create a Ponder token. If an instant is discard this way, you may play it this turn.

**Oscilio, Scion of the Third Age:**
> **Instant** - {r}, {t}, destroy a Lightning Flow you control: Discard a card and create a Ponder token. If an instant is discard this way, you may play it this turn.

**Verdance:**
> If there are 4 or more Earth cards in your banished zone, Verdance gets "Whenever you gain {h} during your turn, you may deal 1 arcane damage to any opposing target."  
> **Essence of Earth**

**Verdance, Thorn of the Rose:**
> If there are 8 or more Earth cards in your banished zone, Verdance gets "Whenever you gain {h} during your turn, you may deal 1 arcane damage to any opposing target."  
> **Essence of Earth**

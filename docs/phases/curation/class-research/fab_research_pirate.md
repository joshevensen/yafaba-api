## Class Identity

**Mechanical theme:** Not covered in fabtcg.com's Jump Start article — this identity is inferred directly from card text rather than official commentary, so treat it as a lighter-confidence read than the other classes. **Gold tokens** are the unmistakable throughline: every playable Pirate hero here (Marlynn, Puffin, Gravy Bones, Scurv) either creates or destroys a Gold token as a core cost/resource, functioning as a class-wide secondary currency layered on top of the normal resource system.

**Recurring patterns:** Gold-token economy is universal in this small roster. Notably, **3 of 4 playable Pirate heroes are dual-class** (Gravy Bones/Necromancer, Marlynn/Ranger, Puffin/Mechanologist) — only Scurv, Stowaway is Pirate-only. This makes Pirate less a standalone identity and more a shared resource layer bolted onto other classes; worth flagging clearly in the quiz so it doesn't feel redundant with three other class options.

## Ability Text

**Gravy Bones:**
> **Instant** - {t}, destroy a Gold you control: Draw a card, then discard a card.  
> If a blue card has been put into your graveyard this turn, you may play cards with watery grave from your graveyard.

**Gravy Bones, Shipwrecked Looter:**
> **Instant** - {t}, destroy a Gold you control: Draw a card, then discard a card.  
> If a blue card has been put into your graveyard this turn, you may play cards with watery grave from your graveyard.

**Marlynn:**
> **Action** - {t}, destroy a Gold you control: Create a Goldfin Harpoon in your hand. **Go again**  
> Whenever you draw a card during your action phase, you may put an arrow from your hand face-up into your arsenal.

**Marlynn, Treasure Hunter:**
> **Action** - {t}, destroy a Gold you control: Create a Goldfin Harpoon in your hand. **Go again**  
> Whenever you draw a card during your action phase, you may put an arrow from your hand face-up into your arsenal.

**Puffin:**
> **Action** - {t}, destroy a Gold you control: Create a Golden Cog token.  
> The second time you crank each turn, draw a card.

**Puffin, Hightail:**
> **Action** - {t}, destroy a Gold you control: Create a Golden Cog token.  
> The second time you crank each turn, draw a card.

**Scurv, Stowaway:**
> **Action** - {t}, destroy a Gold you control: Create a Goldkiss Rum token. **Go again**  
> Whenever you activate a Goldkiss Rum, gain {r}.

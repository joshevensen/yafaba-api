## Class Identity

**Mechanical theme:** Per fabtcg.com's Jump Start article: *"The Mechanologist, Dash, can also relate to the long plans with interesting items and strong defensive cards. However, Dash can also present many attacks in a turn with cards that boost... This type of strategy can make it difficult for an opponent to block correctly and efficiently."* The class's signature resource is the **item** — Mechanologist decks build around playing/recurring items (Data Doll MKII, Dash's item-from-top-of-deck), and around **Evo** cards (Teklovossen's whole kit) and the **crank**/steam-counter/boost subsystems (Maxx Nitro, Puffin).

**Recurring patterns:** playing cards from the top of your own deck as an extra resource (Dash line), banished-zone recursion of Evos (Teklovossen line), and turn-count-based payoffs (Puffin's "second time you crank").

## Ability Text

**Dash:**
> You may start the game with a Mechanologist item with cost 2 or less in the arena.

**Dash I/O:**
> You may look at the top card of your deck at any time.  
> Once per turn, you may play a Mechanologist item with cost 0 or 1 from the top of your deck as though it were an instant. It costs an additional {r} to play.

**Dash, Database:**
> You may look at the top card of your deck at any time.  
> Once per turn, you may play a Mechanologist item with cost 0 or 1 from the top of your deck as though it were an instant. It costs an additional {r} to play.

**Dash, Inventor Extraordinaire:**
> You may start the game with a Mechanologist item with cost 2 or less in the arena.

**Data Doll MKII:**
> Whenever a Mechanologist item with cost 2 or less is put into your banished zone from your deck, put it into the arena.

**Maxx 'The Hype' Nitro:**
> **Once per Turn Action** - {r}{r}: Create a Hyper Driver token with 2 steam counters.  Activate this ability only if you've boosted this turn.  
> Hyper Drivers you control get **crank**.

**Maxx Nitro:**
> **Once per Turn Action** - {r}{r}: Create a Hyper Driver token with 2 steam counters.  Activate this ability only if you've boosted this turn.  
> Hyper Drivers you control get **crank**.

**Professor Teklovossen:**
> Evos cost {r} less to play for each opposing hero.  
> You may play Evos from your banished zone.

**Puffin:**
> **Action** - {t}, destroy a Gold you control: Create a Golden Cog token.  
> The second time you crank each turn, draw a card.

**Puffin, Hightail:**
> **Action** - {t}, destroy a Gold you control: Create a Golden Cog token.  
> The second time you crank each turn, draw a card.

**Teklovossen:**
> You may play Evos from your banished zone.  
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {r}{r}{r}: You may play your next Evo this turn as though it were an instant. When you do, draw a card.

**Teklovossen, Esteemed Magnate:**
> You may play Evos from your banished zone.  
> **Once per Turn Instant** - {r}{r}{r}: You may play your next Evo this turn as though it were an instant. When you do, draw a card.

## Class Identity

**Mechanical theme:** Per fabtcg.com's Jump Start article, contrasting two very different approaches within one class: *"Riptide also sneaks in damage... with specific defense reactions called traps. This is a new and unconventional way to play Ranger, as opposed to Azalea, who buffs up and dominates arrows each turn."* The **arsenal** (the face-down card zone) is the class's core resource — nearly every hero here manipulates what's in arsenal, whether turning it face-up for value (Azalea, Lexi) or hiding cards there as traps (Riptide).

**Recurring patterns:** arsenal manipulation is universal across this small roster; Marlynn is dual-class (Ranger + Pirate, see Pirate file) and Riptide's trap sub-theme has no other class overlap in this dataset — a genuinely distinct niche.

## Ability Text

**Azalea:**
> **Once per Turn Action** - 0: Put a card from your arsenal on the bottom of your deck. If you do, put the top card of your deck face up into your arsenal. If it's an arrow card, it gains **dominate** until end of turn. **Go again**

**Azalea, Ace in the Hole:**
> **Once per Turn Action** - 0: Put a card from your arsenal on the bottom of your deck. If you do, put the top card of your deck face up into your arsenal. If it's an arrow card, it gains **dominate** until end of turn. **Go again**

**Lexi:**
> **Essence of Ice and Lightning**  
> **Once per Turn Action** - Turn a face down card in your arsenal face up: If it's a Lightning card, your next attack this turn gains **go again**. If it's an Ice card, create a Frostbite token under target hero's control. **Go again**

**Lexi, Livewire:**
> **Essence of Ice and Lightning**  
> **Once per Turn Action** - Turn a face down card in your arsenal face up: If it's a Lightning card, your next attack this turn gains **go again**. If it's an Ice card, create a Frostbite token under target hero's control. **Go again**

**Marlynn:**
> **Action** - {t}, destroy a Gold you control: Create a Goldfin Harpoon in your hand. **Go again**  
> Whenever you draw a card during your action phase, you may put an arrow from your hand face-up into your arsenal.

**Marlynn, Treasure Hunter:**
> **Action** - {t}, destroy a Gold you control: Create a Goldfin Harpoon in your hand. **Go again**  
> Whenever you draw a card during your action phase, you may put an arrow from your hand face-up into your arsenal.

**Riptide:**
> Whenever you play a card from hand, you may put a card from hand face down into your arsenal.  
> Whenever a trap you control triggers, deal 1 damage to the attacking hero.

**Riptide, Lurker of the Deep:**
> Whenever you play a card from hand, you may put a card from hand face down into your arsenal.  
> Whenever a trap you control triggers, deal 1 damage to the attacking hero.
